# Engineering Principles

The following principles consistently guided my work:

- Understand the business before designing the solution.
- Modernize incrementally.
- Preserve production stability.
- Measure before optimizing.
- Prefer clear service boundaries.
- Design for observability.
- Treat AI as part of the platform, not a separate product.
- Leave systems easier to understand than when I found them.

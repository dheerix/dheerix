# 09 - Platform Glossary

# Enterprise Commerce & Search Glossary

> A reference guide for the most important concepts, technologies, and terminology used throughout the Holland & Barrett platform.

---

# A

## Aggregation

A search operation that summarizes matching documents rather than returning individual results.

Example:

- Products per Brand
- Products per Category

---

## Analyzer

An Elasticsearch component responsible for converting searchable text into tokens.

Typical operations include:

- Lowercasing
- Stop word removal
- Stemming

---

## API

An interface allowing applications to communicate through well-defined contracts.

---

## ANTLR

Another Tool for Language Recognition.

A parser generator used to implement Domain Specific Languages (DSLs).

Used in the Promotion Engine for parsing complex business rules.

---

# B

## Backend

Server-side services responsible for business logic, APIs, and integrations.

---

## Business Rule

Logic representing business behavior rather than technical implementation.

Example:

> Spend $50 and receive 10% off.

---

# C

## Catalog

The collection of all products available for sale.

---

## Commerce Platform

The collection of services enabling online shopping, including:

- Product Catalog
- Search
- Pricing
- Inventory
- Promotions

---

## CMS

Content Management System.

Used by marketing teams to manage pages and campaigns.

Example:

Magnolia CMS.

---

# D

## DSL

Domain Specific Language.

A language designed for solving problems within a specific domain.

In this platform, DSLs represented promotion rules.

---

# E

## Elasticsearch

A distributed search engine optimized for:

- Full-text search
- Fast retrieval
- Filtering
- Aggregations
- Relevance scoring

---

## Endpoint

A publicly exposed API operation.

Example:

GET /search

---

# F

## Facet

A grouped summary of search results that allows customers to refine searches.

Example:

Brand

- H&B
- Solgar
- Nature's Bounty

---

## Filter

A condition restricting search results.

Examples:

- Brand
- Category
- Price
- Availability

---

# I

## Index

A searchable collection of documents stored within Elasticsearch.

---

## Inventory

The system responsible for tracking product availability.

---

# M

## Merchandising

Business strategies used to influence product visibility.

Examples:

- Boost seasonal products
- Hide discontinued products
- Promote new arrivals

---

# O

## OneSearch

The enterprise product discovery platform.

Responsibilities included:

- Search APIs
- Query construction
- Search orchestration
- Elasticsearch integration

---

# P

## Pagination

Dividing large result sets into smaller pages.

---

## Product Discovery

Helping customers locate products through:

- Search
- Navigation
- Filters
- Recommendations

---

## Promotion Engine

A service responsible for evaluating promotional business rules.

---

# Q

## Query

A search request submitted to Elasticsearch.

---

# R

## React

Frontend framework used to build customer-facing commerce applications.

---

## Relevance

The process of ranking search results according to how well they satisfy customer intent.

---

# S

## Search API

Backend service exposing product search functionality.

---

## Search Platform

A collection of services responsible for product discovery.

---

## Search Relevance

The quality of search result ordering.

---

# T

## Token

A searchable unit of text produced by an analyzer.

---

## Tokenization

The process of splitting text into searchable tokens.

---

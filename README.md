# Dheerix

Dheerix is my personal engineering knowledge system.

It captures the systems I have built, the decisions I have made, the problems I
have solved, the lessons I have learned, and the evidence of my growth as an
engineer.

It is the canonical source for my:

- career knowledge base
- signature projects
- architecture decisions
- engineering stories
- promotion evidence
- interview preparation
- resumes
- public engineering portfolio

## Professional Identity

Senior Software Engineer specializing in production platforms across AI,
distributed systems, cloud technologies, and full-stack product engineering.

I transform complex business requirements into reliable production systems,
taking ownership from problem definition and architecture through implementation,
deployment, observability, and continuous improvement.

## Core Principle

> Build systems, not just features.
